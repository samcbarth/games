Are these games really free?

Yes. Every game is free to play with no sign-up. The site is supported by ads placed outside the play area, never on top of a game and never before you press Play.

Do the games work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Every game has touch controls and resizes to fit your screen. Keyboard controls work on desktop.

Where are high scores stored?

In your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing site data resets them.
