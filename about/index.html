About Lunchbreak Arcade

Lunchbreak Arcade is a small collection of free, original browser games run by Sam Barth. The games are short, work on phones and desktops, and need no download or account.

Who makes the games?

The site is owned and run by Sam Barth. Every game here was written for this site in plain JavaScript and HTML canvas. Genres are familiar (word puzzles, match-three, solitaire, tower defense), but the names, art, rules details and levels are original.

How is the site funded?

Through display advertising. Ads sit outside the play area, never cover a game and never block input. There are no ads before you press Play.

What is Lunchbreak Dispatch?

The site's news section: short write-ups on gaming industry, indie and browser game news. Each story links to its original source, and nothing is reported that the source does not support.

Part of a network

Lunchbreak Arcade is one of several sites on samcbarth.com, listed in the footer of every page.

How can I get in touch?

Use the contact page for bug reports, game ideas or corrections to a news story.
