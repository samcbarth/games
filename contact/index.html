Contact

The fastest way to reach Lunchbreak Arcade is with the form below. Bug reports, game ideas and news corrections are all welcome.

What should a bug report include?

The game name, your device and browser, and what happened just before the problem. A screenshot helps.

Found a mistake in a news story?

Send the story link and the correction with a source. Confirmed errors are fixed and noted on the story.
