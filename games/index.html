All games

There are 10 free games here, each original and playable in any modern browser on desktop or mobile.

Word puzzle

Tangleword

Unscramble jumbled words against a 75 second clock. A free word puzzle game you can play in your browser on phone or desktop.

Match-three puzzle

Tide Pool Swap

Swap shells, pearls and starfish to line up three or more in this free match-three puzzle. 25 moves, chain reactions, no download.

Endless runner

Rooftop Dash

Run across city rooftops at sunset, jump the gaps and dodge vents in this free one-button endless runner for phone and desktop.

Block puzzle

Crate Stack

Drag crate shapes onto an 8 by 8 warehouse floor and clear full rows and columns. A free block puzzle with no timer.

Snake-style arcade

Glowworm

Steer a glowing worm through a night garden, eat dewdrops and grow longer without biting your own tail. Free arcade game.

Logic puzzle

Sinkhole Sweep

Survey a 9 by 9 meadow and mark the 10 hidden sinkholes using number clues. A free logic puzzle in the minesweeper family.

Typing game

Keystorm

Type falling words before they reach the town. A free typing game that gets faster as you go, for keyboards and phones.

Reaction time test

Blink Test

How fast are your reflexes? Tap when the lantern turns green. Five rounds, average reaction time in milliseconds. Free online test.

Solitaire card game

Ridge Solitaire

Clear seven ridges of cards by playing one rank up or down from the pile. A free solitaire card game with simple rules and quick rounds.

Tower defense

Hedge Guard

Plant and upgrade sprinkler towers along a garden path to stop waves of beetles. A free tower defense lite game in your browser.
