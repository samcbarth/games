Rooftop Dash

Rooftop Dash is a one-button endless runner. Your courier sprints across rooftops at dusk, and you decide when to jump. The run speeds up the longer you last, and your score is the distance covered in meters.

Score: 0Best: -

How do you play Rooftop Dash?

  1. Press Play and the courier starts running on their own.
  2. Tap the game, click, or press Space or the Up arrow to jump.
  3. Tap again while in the air for one extra jump. It resets when you land.
  4. Jump over the gaps between buildings and the rooftop vents. Falling into a gap, hitting a taller wall, or clipping a vent ends the run.
  5. The run gets faster over time. Your score is meters run.

What are the controls?

InputAction

Tips for a higher score

  • Save the second jump. Use one jump for most gaps and keep the extra one to correct a short hop.
  • Taller buildings ahead need an earlier jump. If the next roof is higher, jump a little sooner than you think.
  • Watch the far edge of the roof you are on rather than the runner, so you can time the takeoff.
  • Vents only sit on longer rooftops, so a long roof is a cue to look for one.

Rooftop Dash FAQ

How do you score in Rooftop Dash?

Your score is the distance run in meters. It counts up continuously until the run ends.

Is there a double jump?

Yes. You can jump once more while in the air. The second jump is slightly weaker than the first.

Does Rooftop Dash work on mobile?

Yes. Tap anywhere on the game to jump. Landscape orientation gives a bigger view.

Does it get harder?

The speed rises steadily up to a cap, and gaps get wider as you go faster.
