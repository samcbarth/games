Keystorm

Keystorm is a typing game. Words drift down toward a small town, and you type each one to clear it before it lands. The storm speeds up as you go, and three missed words end the round.

Score: 0Best: -

How do you play Keystorm?

  1. Press Play. Words begin falling from the top of the screen.
  2. Type any word you see. Letters you have typed light up on every word that starts with them.
  3. Finish a word exactly and it disappears, scoring 10 points per letter.
  4. Press Space or Enter to clear what you typed if you make a mistake.
  5. A word that reaches the rooftops costs one heart. Lose all three and the round ends.

What are the controls?

InputAction
Letter keysType
BackspaceDelete a letter

Tips for a higher score

  • Clear the lowest word first. A long word near the top can wait.
  • Keep your eyes on the words, not your fingers. The highlight shows which word you are typing.
  • If two words start the same way, keep typing: the one you finish is the one that clears.
  • Accuracy matters more than raw speed. Clearing a typo costs more time than typing carefully.

Keystorm FAQ

Can I play Keystorm on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tap the game to bring up your keyboard. A physical keyboard is faster if you have one.

Does Keystorm help with typing speed?

It is practice in short bursts. Regular play builds familiarity with common letter patterns, but it is a game, not a typing course.

Are capital letters needed?

No. Everything is lowercase and capitals are converted automatically.

How fast does it get?

Words spawn more often and fall faster over time, up to a fixed maximum.
