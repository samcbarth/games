Quebec holds its first Buy a Quebec Video Game Day on November 14

Published November 4, 2026 by Sam Barth

La Guilde du jeu video du Quebec is promoting games made in the province with a one-day event and a portal listing more than 400 titles.

Saturday, November 14, 2026 is the first Buy a Quebec Video Game Day, announced on November 4 by La Guilde du jeu video du Quebec, the province's video game industry guild, working with Rouge marketing.

The event asks players to discover and buy games made in Quebec. Alongside it, the guild launched a portal called Jeux video du Quebec that lists more than 400 games developed in the province.

Why it matters: regional buy-local days are a low-cost way to surface smaller studios before the holiday rush, and the portal stays useful after the day itself.

Source: CNW press release via Yahoo Finance
