Xbox names six Indie Selects for November 2026

Published November 4, 2026 by Sam Barth

The ID@Xbox team picked six indie games for November, including Graveyard Keeper 2, Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 and Ratatan.

Xbox Wire published its Indie Selects list for November 2026 on November 4. Indie Selects is a set of indie games picked by the ID@Xbox team and collected in a hub in the Xbox Store.

This month's six picks are Silver Pines, Graveyard Keeper 2, Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2, Colorbound, Ratatan and Tenebris Somnia. Xbox Wire marks Colorbound, Ratatan and Tenebris Somnia as Xbox Play Anywhere titles, meaning one purchase covers both Xbox console and Windows PC.

Why it matters: curated store lists like this are one of the few ways smaller games get front-page placement on a console storefront.

Source: Xbox Wire
