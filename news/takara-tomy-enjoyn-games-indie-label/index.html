Toy maker Takara Tomy launches Enjoyn Games, an indie publishing label

Published November 4, 2026 by Sam Barth

Takara Tomy has entered game publishing with Enjoyn Games and three party titles for Steam, starting with a burger-stacking social deduction game.

Japanese toy company Takara Tomy has launched an indie game publishing brand called Enjoyn Games, Automaton reported on November 4, 2026. The label announced three games for Steam.

The debut title, Stack & Sus, is a physics-based social deduction game for 3 to 6 players who work together to stack a giant burger while hidden Spies try to sabotage it. The other two are Little Cheese Works, a voice-chat co-op game about mice working at a cheese company without alerting a cat, and Sloppy Forgeries, a painting party game where players recreate classic artworks with limited tools and are scored pixel by pixel.

Why it matters: a large toy company putting its name behind small party games is a sign that indie publishing still looks attractive to companies outside the games business.

Source: Automaton
