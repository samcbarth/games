Xsolla buys GamePix, a browser games platform with 10,000+ titles

Published October 21, 2026 by Sam Barth

Commerce company Xsolla has acquired GamePix, which publishes and distributes web games and ports PC and mobile titles to the browser.

Xsolla, a company that sells payment and commerce tools to game developers, announced on October 21, 2026 that it has acquired GamePix, a platform that publishes and distributes browser games. The announcement says GamePix brings a catalog of more than 10,000 web titles and a community of over 4,000 developers. No price was disclosed.

GamePix was founded in 2013, when web games still ran on Flash, and has since moved through HTML5, WebGL and WebGPU. Xsolla says GamePix's porting technology converts Unity and C++ games for PC and mobile into web builds up to 90% smaller in load size. The release adds that GamePix has been part of Xsolla since August 2026.

Why it matters: browser games are getting more attention from bigger companies again. For players, the pitch is the same one this site is built on: no download, nothing to install, play in a tab.

Source: Business Wire (Xsolla press release)
